Your Hollow Knight: Silksong game, live

Pharloom Calculator will read your Silksong save file and show what the game doesn't tell you: what's missing for 100%, item by item, where every Mask Shard, Spool Fragment and Tool you don't have yet is, on the game's own map, and how many Hunter's Journal entries are left. In Chrome and Edge it will follow the file while you play, and catch up every time you rest at a bench.

It will work without a save too: the Crest screen tells you how each of Hornet's stats changes with each Tool, Crest and upgrade (Needle damage, hits per enemy through its own modifiers, silk, Bind, Silk Skills), and the combat simulator takes your build against every enemy and boss. Every number comes from the wiki, with the game's rounding, and every name from its official translation.

In the making. The study behind it and the plan are public, in the repository.
