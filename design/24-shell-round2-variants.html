Your Hollow Knight: Silksong game, live

Pharloom Calculator reads your Silksong save file and shows what the game doesn't tell you: what's missing for 100%, piece by piece, where every Mask Shard, Spool Fragment and Tool you don't have yet is, on the game's own map, and how many Hunter's Journal entries are left. Its 100% is the game's own: checked on 92 real saves. In Chrome and Edge it follows the file while you play.

Import your save ›

It works without a save too: the Crest screen tells you how each of Hornet's stats changes with each Tool, Crest and upgrade, and Combat takes your build against every enemy, boss and enemy gauntlet, through each one's own modifiers. Every number comes from the wiki, with the game's rounding, and every name from its official translation.

Questions

Is it free? Do I need an account?

Free, no account and no ads. Your save never leaves your browser: it's read there and kept there.

Does it work with the console version?

Reading the save needs the file, and only the computer version (Windows, macOS, Linux, Steam Deck) keeps it where you can reach it. Without a save, the calculator, the map and the Journal work all the same.
